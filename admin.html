Approval & Schedule Automation

When you click Approve on any application:

  • The loan status is updated to Approved with your review remarks.
  • A complete monthly amortization schedule is generated immediately into the payments table.
  • Monthly installment due dates are calculated automatically (1 month, 2 months, ..., N months from approval).
  • The borrower can immediately view their installment table and make payments.
Current interest policy: 10% Fixed Annual Interest Rate for all approved loans.
